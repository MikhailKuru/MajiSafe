Welcome to MajiSafe

Check disaster risks or find the nearest drinkable water source. MajiSafe Mozambique is a lightweight, mobile-friendly disaster alert and water-safety app designed to support communities in Mozambique and across Africa. The app provides real-time disaster alerts, including floods, cyclones, storms, and other emergencies, using live data from trusted sources. Users can quickly check risk levels by entering their province and district, and receive clear safety guidance based on current conditions. In addition, MajiSafe includes practical water conservation tips and a map of nearby drinkable water sources, helping users access safe water even in low-connectivity environments. Built as a Progressive Web App (PWA), MajiSafe works offline, supports installation on mobile devices, and delivers timely notifications so users stay informed when it matters most.

💧 Ways to Conserve Water

  • Fix leaking taps and pipes
  • Collect rainwater for cleaning and gardening
  • Turn off taps while brushing teeth
  • Use buckets instead of hoses
  • Reuse water from washing vegetables
  • Water plants early morning or evening
